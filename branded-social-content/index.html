Lancôme x Olivia Rodrigo

As editor I was handed iPhone footage of Olivia Rodrigo to cut into a 1 minute recap social clip promoting Lancôme’s event collaboration with her. The goal: keep her energy intact while making the video instantly engaging. I worked closely with the social team to shape a fast paced, personality driven edit. The final video reached over 200,000 people and pulled in 17,000+ likes on social.

Role: Editor/ Sound Designer

Project Date: June 2025
Location: New York

Kiehl’s Always On campaign

As Cinematographer for Kiehl’s Always On campaign, a series of vertical social videos were shot aimed for Instagram. The visuals emphasized natural textures, clean composition, and product focus, aligning with Kiehl’s identity while delivering different day in the life scenarios around the product.

Role: Cinematographer

Project Date: March 2025
Location: New York
